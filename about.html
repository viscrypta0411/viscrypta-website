Overview

Viscrypta Technology Private Limited is a deep-tech, innovation-driven company dedicated to designing and developing indigenous cybersecurity solutions. Emerging from the prestigious Chips to Startup (C2S) Hackathon, supported by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), the company focuses on next-generation technologies such as post-quantum cryptography, secure edge computing, AI-enabled surveillance, and hardware-rooted encryption.

Our mission is to strengthen India's technological sovereignty through secure, scalable, and compliant solutions built from the ground up with indigenous expertise. Central to this mission is our active development and integration work leveraging the indigenous SHAKTI processor ecosystem to build secure, optimised compute platforms.

Vision
“To become a global leader in quantum-secure cybersecurity and indigenous defence technologies.”
Mission
  • Develop secure, scalable, and future-ready digital infrastructure for next-generation applications.
  • Contribute to Aatmanirbhar Bharat by driving indigenous innovation in both hardware and software ecosystems.
  • Design and develop fully in-house semiconductor chips in India for defence and surveillance applications, ensuring strategic autonomy.
  • Deliver trusted, high-performance solutions for defence, government, and critical infrastructure with a focus on reliability and security.
  • Ensure data privacy and cyber resilience through advanced cryptographic and post-quantum security technologies.
  • Leverage and contribute to the SHAKTI processor ecosystem, building secure and optimised indigenous compute platforms for edge and embedded systems.
